Complete Feature Breakdown

11 integrated modules covering every store operation

Everything the cashier, inventory manager, and business owner need inside one fast, structured desktop system.

1. High-Speed Point of Sale (POS)

  • Instant background barcode scanning without focusing search + audio confirmation & error tones
  • Smart Arabic normalization search ignoring hamza, taa marbouta, alef maqsura, and diacritics
  • Cashier keyboard shortcuts (/ search, F12 checkout, F9 clear, F2 quick add) + built-in touch keyboard
  • 4 integrated payment methods: Cash, E-Wallet/Card, Split payment, and Credit sale with down payment & credit limit
  • Electronic barcode scale decoding, multi-unit selling (piece/pack/carton), and hold/resume carts
  • Max discount guardrails, automatic tax calculation, negative-stock control, and auto cash-drawer open

2. Credit Customers & Receipt Vouchers (AR)

  • Customer directory with configurable credit limits and protection against deleting indebted accounts
  • Paginated customer ledger tracking credit invoices, receipt vouchers, and credit returns
  • Partial or full debt collection (Cash or Wallet) with a one-click Pay Full Balance action
  • Cash collections automatically flow into the active shift drawer expected cash

3. Shift Handover & Cash Auditing

  • Mandatory shift open with starting float (opening cash) before selling
  • Exact expected cash formula: Opening + Cash Sales + Customer Collections − Supplier Cash Payouts
  • Blind cash count on shift close revealing exact match, shortage, or surplus with cashier notes
  • Manager shift history review with instant notification badge for unreviewed variances

4. Dual Inventory, Transfers & Stock Out

  • Unlimited products and categories with fast pagination and automatic barcode generation
  • Strict separation between Shop Floor and Warehouse balances with cost price hidden from cashiers
  • Minimum stock threshold alerts and expiry date tracking with advance warning windows
  • Additional selling units per product with independent conversion ratios and prices
  • Audited two-way Stock Transfers (Warehouse ↔ Shop) plus Stock Out logging for damaged/expired items

5. Returns & Invoice Refund Management

  • Lookup by original invoice number with automatic return-window validation
  • Partial or full item returns automatically restoring shop stock while preventing duplicate returns
  • Cash, Wallet, or Split refunds — plus automatic balance deduction for credit customer invoices

6. Suppliers & Supply Debt Ledger

  • Supplier directory tracking contact info, account status, and balances with deletion protection
  • Supply invoices automatically increment Warehouse stock and log paid vs. remaining debt
  • Edit recorded supply invoices with automatic stock reconciliation and full Supply Edit History audit trail
  • Supplier cash payouts deduct simultaneously from supplier debt and active shift drawer cash

7. Employees, Salaries & Lateness Log

  • Staff directory with job titles, phone numbers, and base monthly salaries
  • Monthly payroll ledger summarizing base salaries, paid amounts, deductions, and remaining balance
  • Log salary payments, deductions, and lateness records with dates and notes

8. Visual Receipt & PDF Report Designers

  • Block-based Visual Receipt Designer (logo, banner, items table, credit details, barcode/QR) with live preview
  • Block-based Visual Report Designer for customizing printed thermal and PDF sales/profit reports
  • Direct ESC/POS printing on 80mm & 58mm printers over USB or TCP/IP with auto-cut and reconnect
  • High-resolution PDF report export with optional auto-open after saving

9. Executive Dashboard & Financial Reports

  • Real-time KPIs for daily sales, monthly revenue, estimated net profit, supplier balances, and weekly chart
  • Top-bar notification center aggregating low stock, upcoming expiries, and unreviewed shift variances
  • Daily, weekly, monthly, and custom date-range reports with credit sales & collections tracking
  • Top-selling items, slow movers, and per-cashier sales with quick export shortcut (Ctrl+E)

10. Hardware Licensing & 16 Cashier Permissions

  • Hardware-fingerprint licensing supporting both permanent activation and time-limited trial mode
  • Anti-Cloning database lock detecting unauthorized machine copies + emergency security wipe
  • Two-factor TOTP verification for Super Admin login
  • 16 granular cashier permission toggles controlling discounts, returns, supplies, credit sales, and more

11. Settings, One-Click Backup & Themes

  • Centralized store profile, currency, tax ID, discount limits, return window, and printer settings
  • One-click full database Backup & Restore for hardware fault protection
  • Full bilingual Arabic (RTL) & English (LTR) interface with Light and Dark modes
